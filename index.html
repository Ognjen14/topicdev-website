How we build

Quality first, in every app

What goes into every TopicDev app, and what it looks like app by app.

01

Made properly, down to the details

Native code that opens fast and stays smooth, a design made for each screen it runs on, and features that are finished before they ship. You can see it in every app:

  • MakimediaYour films on every screen at home
  • RerollOne pick at a time, and where it streams
  • MD EditorClean reading, split editing, live math
  • Topic FinanceBudgets that warn you, in any currency
  • C++ GuideFrom first lesson to senior interview
  • Python GuideLearn by doing, with real exercises
02

Your data, on your device

Your library, lists, budgets and progress are kept on your own device. Where an app offers something in the cloud, like a backup, it's off until you turn it on.

  • MakimediaYour library lives on your PC and streams to your screens at home.
  • RerollYour watchlist and settings stay on your phone.
  • MD EditorYour files never leave your PC.
  • Topic FinanceOn your phone. Drive backup and the shared budget stay off until you turn them on.
  • C++ and Python guidesYour progress stays on your phone.
03

Fair prices, and no ads

Two apps are completely free. The others start free or with a free trial, and you always see the price in the store before you pay. None of them shows ads, in a free version or after you pay.

  • FreeMakimedia and RerollFree and open source, under the GNU GPL v3.
  • Pay onceMD EditorA one-time purchase in the Microsoft Store. Still open source, under the GNU GPL v3.
  • Pay onceC++ and Python guidesFree to start. Pro is a single payment that's yours to keep.
  • Free trialTopic FinanceEvery feature free for 21 days, then Premium by the month, the year or once for life.

Reroll

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

In short: Reroll collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your watchlist and settings stay on your device. Reroll talks to TMDB for film and show details, and to YouTube when you play a trailer.

Who we are

Reroll is an Android app made by TopicDev. In this policy, "we" means TopicDev.

Information we collect

None. Reroll doesn't collect, receive, send, sell or share any personal information, and we have no server that could receive it. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Reroll keeps the following only on the device you use it on:

  • your watchlist
  • the titles you marked as watched, with your progress through episodes
  • titles you skipped or hid
  • your filters and settings
  • cached posters and images

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling Reroll.

Third-party services

TMDB (The Movie Database). Reroll gets titles, posters, descriptions, ratings, release dates and where a title is streaming from the TMDB API. Your device connects to TMDB directly, so TMDB receives your IP address and basic details of each request. See the TMDB privacy policy.

JustWatch. The information about where a title is streaming is provided to TMDB by JustWatch. Reroll gets it through TMDB and never connects to JustWatch itself.

YouTube. When you play a trailer, it comes from YouTube, which receives the same kind of request information. See the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

Reroll asks only for the internet permission, to fetch film and show details and to load trailers.

Children

Reroll isn't directed at children. It shows information about films and shows for adults, which may include mature content.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request. For data held by TMDB or Google, please contact them directly.

Security

Since nothing is sent to us, there is no data of yours on our side to breach. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date, and the change will be mentioned in the release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@topicdev.com, or the contact form.

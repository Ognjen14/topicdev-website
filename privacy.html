Website privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

In short: this website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It only learns about you if you write to us through the contact form. Each app has its own privacy policy.

This website

topicdev.com is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from advertising or tracking services. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The contact form

When you send a message, the form passes your name (if you give it), your email address, the app and topic you chose and your message to Web3Forms, which emails it to us. Web3Forms also includes your IP address in that email. We use your details only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

The apps

Each app treats your data differently, so each one has its own privacy policy, inside the app and, as they're published here, on the app's page.
